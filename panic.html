Add this to your home screen
iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): tap the menu, then Install app or Add to home screen.
Warning
Opening this icon counts as tap 1. Tap the screen once more within 2.5 seconds: your contacts get your location. Tap twice more: siren, until you enter your PIN. If you do nothing, nothing happens.

Set up your contacts and PIN in the main app first: open the app.
